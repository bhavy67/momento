Momento Brand Identity
The Idea
The Ring & The Gem
A mark that means something — not just a letter in a circle.

Form follows feeling

The 300° arc is a ring — both the jewellery you give someone you love, and the cycle of a year. Dates come back. Time is circular, not linear. The arc carries almost a full year in its sweep.

The gap at 12 o'clock is the moment: the exact instant a year resets, where your reminder lives, where the important date falls. Not a void — an opening in time.

The gem in the gap is the memento itself. Set like a stone in a ring, it turns an abstract arc into something precious — a kept object, a marker that says this moment matters. The word "momento" is Latin for memory; the dot is the memory, held in the ring of time.
